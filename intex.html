Formula & Learning Center

Search formulas, understand variables and use your own values.

Apparent Power

S = V × I

S = apparent power (VA), V = voltage (V), I = current (A).

Active Power

P = V × I × PF

PF is the power factor. Result is in watts.

Reactive Power

Q = √(S² − P²)

Q is reactive power in VAR.

Power Factor

PF = P / S

Ratio of active power to apparent power.

Energy

E(kWh) = P(W) × t(hours) / 1000

Energy is power consumed over time.

Total Active Power

Ptotal = PR + PY + PB

Voltage Imbalance

Imbalance % = MAX(|Vphase − Vavg|) / Vavg × 100
Vavg = (VR + VY + VB) / 3

Current Imbalance

Imbalance % = MAX(|Iphase − Iavg|) / Iavg × 100

Phase Load Distribution

Phase Load % = Phase Power / Total Power × 100

Overall Power Factor

PFoverall = Ptotal / Stotal

Quick Theory

Power Triangle: S² = P² + Q²

Active power performs useful work. Reactive power supports magnetic and electric fields. Apparent power represents the combined electrical demand.
